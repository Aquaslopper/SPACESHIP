# HTA lines 2001–4000 — gameplay logic and geometry spotting

> **Follow-up clarification:** after this review the user confirmed that HTA-authored cooldown tiers are correct; five-action repairs are not a blanket override. The original N58 policy-conflict verdict below is therefore superseded. See [the follow-up repair record](lines_2001_4000_repairs.md) for the authorized changes and the scan-formula evidence. ## Scope and evidence

Notes only, reviewed against native commit `55143fd`. No game code, data, assets, builds, tests, commits, or fixes were made for this pass. The companion `lines_2001_4000_line_ledger.csv` retains each of the 2,000 physical source lines and links it to these observations.

**PRESENT** means an identifiable source-level implementation, not live gameplay or GPU proof. **PARTIAL/MISSING** identifies a specific difference. **POLICY CONFLICT** means the port agrees with the HTA text but conflicts with the user's later correction. **BOUNDARY** means a limited edge/API difference, not a demonstrated ordinary-voyage failure. **DEFERRED** means later source extensions are needed to decide full parity. **PLATFORM/SCOPE** separates implementation details from gameplay rules.

The approved 18 px text floor remains. Positive-click feedback is not restricted to clicks that spend actions. The user's five-other-committed-action correction for one-action controls takes precedence over literal HTA tiers. Hull, small rooms, and room heroes remain beta; this review does not authorize redesigning them. The previous notes describe their earlier review snapshot; this document inspects the subsequently committed implementation.

## Important distinctions for the next discussion

### Concrete differences to discuss, not fixes made

1. **One-action cooldown policy is still not consistently implemented.** Routine is five; other native categories retain ten/fifteen/twenty.
